FAQ

Before you ask

Still unsure? Send the form — questions are free.

How fast can you shoot?

Usually within 3–5 days, and same-week for listings when the calendar allows.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no cost. In Vancouver, I plan for it.

Does the car need to be clean?

Yes — a wash is the one thing that changes everything. I'll send a short prep list before the shoot.

Where can I use the photos?

Personal and own-channel use is included in every package. Paid advertising and third-party use are quoted separately.

Do you travel outside Vancouver?

Yes — Richmond, Burnaby, Surrey, Coquitlam, the North Shore and the Sea-to-Sky. Longer trips are confirmed in the quote.

Can you shoot a whole batch of inventory?

Yes. Dealership batch rates start at 5 cars per visit.
