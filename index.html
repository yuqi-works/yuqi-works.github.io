02 — About

The Person
Behind The Lens

I've been shooting cars in Vancouver for over a decade — from weekend meet-ups and canyon runs to the grid of the Diamond Rally, where I served as staff photographer. A commercial drone licence means I'm not just parked at ground level: the shot above the car matters as much as the one at bumper height.

Shooting with Sony full-frame — A7R III stills, FX3/FX6 and Z-CAM E2-F6 for motion — from canyon runs to car shows, I chase the frame that makes metal feel alive: paint under light, motion frozen, the environment doing half the work.

Based in Vancouver BC. Available for events, launches, track days, and portrait-style car features. If you like my work, feel free to share it — credit is always appreciated.
